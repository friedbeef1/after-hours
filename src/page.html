The room comes alive

v8 BRIGHT TOY ROOM - first playable. Original opponent AI and match rules. New living-room layout; navigation and map balance in testing. Applewood Mill retains its original scenery.

Game

Two toy crews, 5 a side. Nobody gets hurt, just very messy.

  • Hide itCarry the biscuit to toy box A or B and hold E to hide it. Guard the drop until the timer ends to win.
  • Retrieve itDefenders win by retrieving the biscuit, or keeping it from reaching a toy box in time.
  • Shop smartWin rounds and splat opponents to earn buttons. Teams swap jobs after 5 rounds. First to 6 wins.
